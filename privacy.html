Tánqí — Privacy Policy

Developer: Nexaro Apps · Android app (com.nexaro.tanqi) · Last updated: 20 November 2026

Tánqí (“the app”) is a game published by Nexaro Apps (“we”). This policy explains what data is processed when you play the Android version.

No account, and we do not collect personal data

The app needs no sign-in and has no servers of its own. We do not collect, receive or sell your name, e-mail address, contacts, location or other personal information. There is no first-party analytics or crash reporting.

Your progress, settings, coins, unlocks and purchase flags are stored only on your device (in the app’s local storage). Android backup is enabled, so a device backup may include that local save. Uninstalling the app, or clearing the app’s storage, deletes this local data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app may show ads through Google AdMob, provided by Google: banners during a match, interstitials after some matches, and optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device’s advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • general device information (model, operating system, language, IP address, and approximate location derived from the IP address);
  • interactions with ads (views and taps) and diagnostic data.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google’s consent tool. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Google’s use of data is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

You can remove banners and interstitials with a one-time in-app purchase (rewarded videos remain optional).

In-app purchases (Google Play)

Optional purchases are sold through Google Play Billing:

  • a one-time “Remove ads” purchase (non-consumable; also grants bonus coins and can be restored);
  • consumable coin packs used only inside the game.

Payment is handled entirely by Google; we never receive your card or bank details. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was made, so the app can deliver or restore it on your device. See Google’s privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Optional local notifications

If you opt in (Settings → Notifications), the app may schedule reminders on your device, for example when a chest is ready, when the daily spin resets, or to help you keep a streak. These notifications are created locally on your device and are not sent through a Tánqí server. You can turn them off at any time in the app or in your Android settings.

Internet use

The Android app uses the internet for Google Play Billing and, when ads are shown, for Google AdMob. We do not operate a first-party game server.

Children

The game is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask for access, correction or deletion of personal data and you may complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens). Because we hold no personal data ourselves, clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the app deletes everything we store. For data processed by Google (ads or purchases), use Google’s privacy settings or contact Google. Questions: nexaroapps@gmail.com.
